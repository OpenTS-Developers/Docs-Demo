Actions executed after a trigger's events are satisfied.

#NameEditor summaryParameters
0-No Action-This is a null action. It will do nothing and is equivalent to not having an action at all. Why use it?None
1Winner is...The winner will be forced to be the house specified. The game will end immediately. Typically, the player's house is specified.House
2Loser is...The loser will be force to be the house specified. The game will end immediately. Typically, the player's house is specified.House
3Production Begins...The computer's house (as specified) will begin production of units and structures.House
4Create Team...Creates a team of the type specified (owned by the house of this trigger). The team member are NOT automatically created however.Team
5Destroy Team...Destroys all instances of the team type specified. The units in those existing teams will remain and be available for recruiting into other teams.Team
6All to Hunt...Forces all units, of the house specified, into 'hunt' mode. They will seek out and destroy their enemies.House
7Reinforcement (team)...Create a reinforcement of the specified team. The members of the team WILL be created magically by this action.Team
8Drop Zone Flare (waypoint)...Display a drop zone flair at the waypoint specified. The map will also be reaveald around that location.Waypoint
9Fire Sale...Cause all buildings of the specified house to be sold (for cash and prizes). Typically this is used in the final assault by the computer.House
10Play Movie...Displays the specified movie (full screen). The game is paused while this occurs and resumes normally after it completes.Movie
11Text Trigger...Display the text identified by the string file <label>.Number
12Destroy Trigger...Destroy all current instances of the trigger type specified. This does not prevent future instances of that trigger >from being created.Trigger
13Autocreate Begins...Initiates autocreate for the house specified. This will cause the computer's house to build autocreate teams as it sees fit.House
14Change House...Changes owning house to the one specified for attached objects.House
15Allow WinRemoves one 'blockage' from allowing the player to win. The blockage number is equal the number of triggers created that have this action.None
16Reveal all mapReveals the entire map to the player.None
17Reveal around waypoint...Reveals a region of the map to the player around the waypoint specified.Waypoint
18Reveal zone of waypoint...Reveals all cells that share the same zone as the waypoing specified. This yields some wierd results. Use with caution.Waypoint
19Play sound effect...Plays the sound effect specified.Sound
20Play music theme...Plays the music theme specified.Theme
21Play speech...Plays the speech sound specified.EVA speech

26Timer Shorten...Short the global mission timer by the time specified. It can never be reduced below 'zero' time.Number
27Timer Set...Set the global mission timer to the value specified.Number

30Auto Base Building...Initialize the computer skirmish mode build control to either 'on' or 'off' state. When 'on', the computer takes over as if it were in skirmish mode. (gs make sure he has a con yard)Boolean
31Grow shroud one 'step'Increase the shroud darkness by one step (cell).None
32Destroy attached buildingDestroy any buildings, bridges, or units that this trigger is attached to.None
